## OWNR

OWNR is a UK company that provides **QR-code lost property labels** for schools, sports clubs, and families. Its service is designed to help misplaced uniforms, PE kits, water bottles, lunchboxes, and other belongings find their way back to their owners while keeping personal contact details private. ([OWNR][1])

### What the company offers

OWNR sells **iron-on** and **stick-on** labels that each contain a unique QR code. After activating the labels, users attach them to clothing or equipment. If an item is found, the finder scans the QR code with a smartphone and can send a secure message through OWNR's platform, allowing the owner to arrange its return without exposing their phone number or address. No app is required for the finder. ([OWNR][2])

### Privacy and design

A key feature of the service is its emphasis on **privacy**. According to the company, the QR codes do not reveal personal contact information, communications are relayed securely, and the platform is designed to comply with UK GDPR requirements. The labels are marketed as durable enough for everyday school use, including machine washing for iron-on labels and water-resistant use for stick-on versions. ([OWNR][2])

### Schools and clubs

Beyond individual families, OWNR targets schools and sports organizations. The company promotes a fundraising model in which participating schools or clubs can receive **20% of qualifying purchases** made through their campaign, with no inventory or administrative setup required from the organization. ([OWNR][1])

### Positioning

OWNR focuses on reducing the volume of unclaimed lost property by combining **physical name labels** with **digital QR-based contact**. Rather than using Bluetooth tracking or requiring dedicated hardware, its system depends on someone finding the item and scanning the printed code, making it suitable for everyday belongings that are commonly misplaced in schools and sports settings. ([OWNR][1])
